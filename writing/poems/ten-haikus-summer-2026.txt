plunge headlong black tar
telepathic twine prick my
spine heroin eyes

and your arm is mine
in mooring steadfast against
a current of dreams

young blood in office
doused in cobalt blue linens
naked and afraid

hush baby cobra
amber tan on white hot skin
softens my edges

powell has suspenders
adam and god have a gap
batman has robin

looked around my room
and saw only greenery
in the foggy light

wispy fishes skirt
statues embalmed by storied
pyroclastic flow

my worn ballet flats
carry me through new york like
blue bullets singing

two mutts sit alone
amidst a man's belongings
like good patient boys

leopard spots black flip
flops and a book by bell hooks
light love at my heels
