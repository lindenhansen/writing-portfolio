Today is the first day I’m having a big girl birthday,
and here are the things I know about me:

I’m most me with beer, bootcuts and a dream,
dirty jeans, blond streaked,
letting light in through the eaves,

indifferent nature,
the Bible, root vegetables
and asking why,

cappuccinos with chocolate powder,
red lips and sexiest dancer,
dirt roads in a sagebrush sea,

no room to call mine,
up first with the sun,
two broken hearts and another one,

the horizon line,
boys with squinty kind eyes,
friendships with dawn and dewy blades of grass,

brazen drum crash,
scent of rice, tamarind, and jasmine,
far more yesses than nos,

inability to speak without smiling,
lavender and champagne, lack of hair brush,
writing to live, even though

it took me 21 years to find out
“You’re more of a poet!”
The woman, red-scarved and white-wined

told me Sunday, at the pub.
And you know what,
she was right.
