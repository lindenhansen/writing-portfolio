It always smells good here.
An evergreen aroma
of sagebrush, pine, and
something else like warmth.
A yellow, purple and grey kind of scent
explained only as
the feeling of laying in a bed of prairie smoke.
The sonic landscape bears a brooding edge colored by
the constant hum of beetles,
delivering the listener
into delirium.

I am sinking into the hammock's sway,
looking up
as a hummingbird flits across
my frame of gaze,
from one aspen to another—
two trees anchor me here.
Is he clued into nature's telepathic pulse?
The eyelets copied from identical strands.
Inscrutable biological instruction,
but still, winking differently.

My knuckles are translucent white this morning,
burned from carrying
the firepit to the creek.
Embers faded out into quiet
flickers of light
floating up into the largest black sky.
All the way up until
indistinguishable from stars.
I trace a mental imprint of Orion's Belt,
recall the bubble of water
under the sounds of low voices,
the feeling of hot
flames on my sunburned face, and
hard questions pierce my heart.

I love you Linden,
he says, standing in the frame
of the doorway.
He says it like he means it. But also because
he's obligated to leave on a high note.
We argue about blissful ignorance
and nationalism.
Words always marked by cutting sarcasm,
doused in
brutal, brazen, honesty.
You're hot enough
that you will find someone.
Don't tell me
you don't know it.
Unexpectedly protective,
but mocking, too.
Always exceptionally honest,
to a fault. I respect him for it.
We spar and curse each other
and say goodbye.

If my hips didn't curve this way
and my eyes weren't butter brown,
would I still feel the same?

Each time I get off the plane
and lay eyes on those mountains,
my throat catches and my eyes glisten.
Crisp air smacks me in the face and
the triplet of rocky peaks
liberates,
reminds me just how
small I am,
and just how
much I don't know.
Just how
I probably couldn't even figure it out
the answer to that question
if I tried.
I stop picking my nails—
they splinter to pieces
anyways in the dryness,
with
or without my help.
A burden lifted.
