on 27th street I woke up frighteningly alive
tucked my shirt into my jeans
was
birthed into the street and
burned in an instant
by a passerby's lit cigarette

a last meal at the bar stool
of eggs home style potatoes and rye toast
paired well with dark coffee and a metal milk tinge

they asked do I want a refill and of course I exclaimed
yes why ever not?

drums banged to the chime of cutlery clang
and the buzz had me feel fuzzy
and my mind
raced like sellers in the heat

then finally I thought of new york

the black grime
steaming manholes and
fluttering doves on a sill
with wings that whipped in time to a
pattering in my heart that pulled me from my skin
to this and that hood
uptown downtown
forced my hand
to clutch the chrome of a freight train
hurtling through the
pulse of America
electrical wires
crisscrossed the tunneled underbelly
and beat together in a chorus of litanic tremors to cast
a blinking white night on a thousand pounds of
human flesh and blood

the gavel hammered home
sentenced me
on the basis of my own existence
my own belief
of my own existence

it whispered
the eternal ways to flirt with life
turn the corner! lick the cream!
slip in vintage silk and golden luster
eat everything or
eat nothing at all
trace cracked spines and absorb the words
written by others whose conviction
equal in faith
you were taught to disbelieve
rebel against reality
play at consequence

but even after all this weight
in morning light my heart relented
to the soft sound of trees

and still I found a minted green pasture cool
enough to bottle and press into my painter's tin

I left many things and took some too
to pack in the suitcase that collected dust
tenderly
for ten weeks
under my bed

I will keep my black loafers thank you
but surrender my hair to platinum
