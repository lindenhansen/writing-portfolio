i spied a trunk cut in two
through the mist of a mountain creek
sliced so the rings were crisp in the september-leaning air
they whispered

with each flip of the heart
you will find more reason to love
jagged cracks through the bark are familiar not ugly
trust in me

shoulders bear alpine rain
my soaked steps in soil are heavy
steeped like rotten leaves in last spring's shallow promise
far away

my father's words sing
hold two hands tight 'round forgiveness
cling to the good the bad the muddy
white knuckled

ferns here are lonely too
unfurl into green smiles in sun
dappled grin
