fantasies of cappuccinos on ebony stoops
while water webs the windows
elect to croissant and sit and eat and sip

a hole fights the bottom of a blue ballet flat
pierced on Jimi Hendrix way
flying strapless through L.E.S. streets

thoughts I've never before had
splashing on heat in Bangkok
birds here and there sounds the same

today I have time on my side
to think, not down the avenue
electric chords strung round my ears

perhaps a sweet potato perhaps
a bowl of berries and cheese
but a scotch egg waits for me
on the menu off Bleecker

thank God for friends
an ocean of people
breathe ribs wide open
